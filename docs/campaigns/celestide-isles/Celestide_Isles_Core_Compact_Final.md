# **01 Campaign Overview**

## **Campaign Overview: The Celestide Isles**

The Celestide Isles are a chain of floating islands held aloft by ancient crystal currents.

Each island has its own ecosystem, culture, and Gym challenge.

## **Core Themes**

* Discovery & friendship  
* Responsible use of power  
* Learning from mistakes  
* Teamwork across differences

## **Main Goal**

Travel together, defeat Gym Leaders, stop Team Drift, and help complete the regional Pokédex.

# **02 Worldbuilding**

## **Worldbuilding: The Celestide Isles**

## **Geography**

* Floating islands connected by air routes  
* Crystal caverns that alter gravity  
* Warm trade winds and glowing night skies

## **Evil Team: Team Drift**

Team Drift believes the islands should be controlled to prevent disaster.

They are misguided engineers and researchers, not cruel villains.

Goal: Harness gravity crystals to 'stabilize' the region, even if towns must be relocated.
