# Celestide Isles – Expansion Content (Final Compact Edition)

# **07 Session 1 Opening**

## **Session 1: The Day the Sky Shifted**

The players arrive at Skysong Harbor during the annual Wind Festival.

Colorful banners float between buildings as airships dock peacefully.

Suddenly, a gravity crystal destabilizes.

A section of the harbor lifts into the air.

Team Drift operatives appear, claiming this is 'for everyone’s safety.'

This is the party’s first moral test: stop them, help civilians, or gather proof.

## **Technician Mira**

# **09 Gym 1 Battle Rules**

## **Gym 1 Battle: Zephyra’s Trial**

Battle Format:

* 2 Pokémon per trainer  
* Environmental effects: Strong winds

Special Rule:

Each round, Flying-type Pokémon gain \+1 initiative.

Failure Outcome:

Zephyra pauses the battle and teaches strategy before retry.
